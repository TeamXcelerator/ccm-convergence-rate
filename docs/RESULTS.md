## Qualifications retained in the manuscript

- The finite-window stretched transition is an empirical model. The working
  manuscript records failures of its unchanged large-cutoff extrapolation.
- Historical ramp-table entries at `C=150,200` are not covered by the current
  executable grid because their exact original configurations remain to be
  reconciled.
- A precision-floor value can be unresolved even when a process completes.
  Converged, stagnated, approximate, and failed roots are distinct outcomes.
- Reference-seeded refinement tests a requested root neighborhood; it is not
  independent discovery or a complete spectral ordinal count.
- A certificate for a serialized matrix or vector concerns that finite
  source. Transfer to the exact assembled operator, the analytic ground state,
  and the continuum requires the separate qualifications stated in the paper.
- Conditional finite bounds and source-assisted predictors do not establish
  a uniform parameter-only law in `C,N,P,k`.

The later working-manuscript continuation reports additional finite root,
source, and predictor studies. Their own retained definitions and hypotheses
govern them. The numbered scalar reproduction scripts do not rerun all of
those studies or replace their evidence.

## What counts as completion

A current claim needs every planned configuration with the requested source,
precision, root window, numerical policies, and selected diagnostic scope.
Missing or non-finite measurements cannot pass a fit after silently reducing
the grid. Software reports distinguish execution integrity from the finite
hypothesis result and from publication status.

An execution-integrity pass means the required evidence was obtained and
checked; a finite hypothesis may still fail. An unassessed demonstration
cannot become an asymptotic proof. Acquired diagnostics may retain unresolved
rows or conditional bounds, whose scope must be read in the artifact.

For C1, C2, and C3, the explicit `paper3-precision-control-v3` assessment preserves two
coverage results. `capture_complete` and `all_requested_capture_complete`
describe the unchanged raw receipt. `applicable_capture_complete` additionally
recognizes only the declared, source-bound stored-precision limitations of
those controls. The summary's `capture_coverage` lists every row and any
qualified diagnostics. A pass under this policy does not make raw incomplete
Ultra capture complete, and does not establish the ground-dependent hypothesis.
Unknown errors, missing inputs, and exhausted budgets remain incomplete.

For an authorized publication run, the retained inventory and successful
destination-coverage report are required in addition to numerical evidence.
Successful publication by itself does not establish numerical completion.
Ordinary local reproduction publishes nothing.
