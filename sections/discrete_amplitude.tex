\subsection{The exact Fourier boundary and a saturation control}
\label{sec:discrete-amplitude-control}

The next discriminator retains the exact Gamma carrier instead of its
leading interior approximation. Let $y=x/\beta$, $\tau=y/N$, and
\begin{align*}
 V_N(y)&=(N+y)\log\frac{N+y}{N}
            +(N-y)\log\frac{|N-y|}{N},\\
 J_N(y)&=\left|\frac{\Gamma(N+1)^2}
 {\Gamma(N+1+y)\Gamma(N+1-y)}\right|e^{V_N(y)}.
\end{align*}
Here $0\log0=0$ and reciprocal Gamma is continued through its zeros.
Inside $y<N$, Stirling's leading factor is $(1-\tau^2)^{-1/2}$,
which loses uniformity at the last retained Fourier mode. The exact
factor has a finite transition there.

\begin{proposition}[Uniform factorization of the known carrier]
\label{prop:uniform-carrier-factor}
Define the exact positive-real Stirling remainder by
\[
 R(u)=\log\Gamma(u+1)-(u+\tfrac12)\log u+u-\tfrac12\log(2\pi).
\]
For $N\geq1$, $y\geq0$, $h=y-N$, put
$\Phi(h)=e^{h-h\log|h|}|1/\Gamma(1-h)|$, with $\Phi(0)=1$.
Then, including carrier zeros by continuity,
\[
 J_N(y)=\sqrt{\pi N}\,\Phi(h)
       \left(1+\frac{h}{2N}\right)^{-1/2}
       e^{2R(N)-R(N+y)}.
\]
Away from those exact zeros, omitting the final exponential incurs
relative error below $1/(6N)$, uniformly for $y\geq0$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Substitute the defining remainder identity at the two positive arguments
$N$ and $N+y$. All large logarithmic terms cancel to give the displayed
factorization. Binet's formula\cite{DLMFGamma} implies that $R$ is positive and decreasing,
with $R(u)<1/(12u)$. Therefore
$0<2R(N)-R(N+y)<1/(6N)$, and the relative error is
$1-e^{-[2R(N)-R(N+y)]}<1/(6N)$. At a carrier zero both expressions
vanish, so no relative error is assigned.
\end{proof}
At $y=N$ the leading size is $\sqrt{\pi N}$; at
$y=N+1,N+2,\ldots$ the forced carrier zeros remain exact.
For the interior remainder
$E=2R(N)-R(N+y)-R(N-y)$, Binet's integral also gives
\[
 -\frac{y^2}{6N(N^2-y^2)}\leq E\leq0,\qquad 0\leq y<N.
\]
These are estimates of a known factor, not a CCM error bound. The
factorization and signed bounds pass 693 Arb checks at 150 digits,
including both sides of the boundary and exact carrier zeros.

\paragraph{Improved exploratory curves, followed by a failed control.}
Multiplying the previous trial transform by the exact signed Gamma
factor and recomputing its finite lattice sums reduces the five-case
RMS depth errors to $0.216,0.183,0.188,0.114,0.059$ digits, in the
same configuration order as the preceding subsection. Nevertheless,
only 386/4373 displacements pass the unchanged $1\%$ gate.
A separate imaginary-order Bessel transport hypothesis raises this
to 700/4373, still insufficient. It replaces a leading quarter-power
amplitude by
\[
 Q_B(a,x)=\frac{e^{-\pi x/2}\operatorname{Re}I_{ix}(a)}{I_0(a)}
                  e^{-S_a(x)+a},\qquad
 S_a(x)=\sqrt{a^2-x^2}-x\arccos(x/a).
\]
The candidate divides the Gamma-corrected bare displacement by $Q_B$.
The Bessel definition is standard\cite{DLMFImaginaryBessel}; no CCM
differential equation identifying that counterpart is established. Its evaluation
required adaptive precision, reaching 1600 digits. Earlier 90-digit
results with inadequate ball widths are retained as unqualified attempts.

A new control holds $C=125$ fixed and raises $N$ from 1500 to 2000,
keeping the same 2300-digit assembly, 2000-digit point solve, reference
targets and operator-predictor rule. All 1000 point-state roots have
disjoint enclosures in their requested reference windows and a matching
exact polynomial upper count. No carrier lies below the counting
threshold. All 1000 unchanged operator predictions pass; their worst
relative error is $2.51079\cdot10^{-6}$.

\paragraph{A sharper model statement about the minimum mode budget.}
For $x>4\pi$ and $C>x/(4\pi)$, write $\ell_x=\log(x/(4\pi))$.
Equation~\eqref{eq:action-mode-threshold} becomes
\[
 N_{\rm turn}(C,x)=\frac{x\ell_x}{4}\frac{\sin u}{u},
 \qquad u=\frac{\pi\ell_x}{2\log C}.
\]
It increases with $C$, from $x\ell_x/(2\pi)$ at the cutoff boundary
to $x\ell_x/4$ as $C\to\infty$. At fixed $N$, the largest model
endpoint occurs at
\[
 C_* =\frac{N}{2W(N/2)},\qquad
 a_{\max}=\frac{2\pi N}{W(N/2)},
\]
where $W$ is the positive Lambert function. These follow from the
explicit endpoint equation; they are not CCM capacity theorems.
The degree requirement $N\geq k$ remains separate. In the mode-limited
branch, putting $s=\sqrt{a^2-x^2}$ and $b=\sqrt{a^2-A^2}$ gives
\[
 \partial_N\mathcal S=2\log\left|\frac{s+b}{s-b}\right|>0,
 \qquad x<a,\quad x\ne A.
\]
Yet its $C$-derivative need not be positive. Close to the model endpoint,
with $\delta=a-x\ll a-A$,
\[
 \mathcal S(a-\delta)\sim
 \frac{2\sqrt2}{3\sqrt a}
 \left(1+\frac{2LA}{\pi b}\right)\delta^{3/2}.
\]
The cutoff-limited coefficient has bracket one; this expansion is not
uniform when the two boundaries coalesce. Fifty-one independent point
checks verify these model identities. They explain why a useful indexed
minimum must account for distance from the resolution boundary, rather
than merely clip an inaccurate depth to zero. Uniform prefactor,
source-transfer and arithmetic estimates are still required.
