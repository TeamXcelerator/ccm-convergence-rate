\subsection{Exact joint transport under a mode increment}
\label{ssec:joint-mode-transport}

The failed saturation control points to an exact finite replacement for
an isolated carrier correction. Let the next even matrix be
$M=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}A&b\\b^T&d\end{smallmatrix}\right)$,
with $M>0$, and extend the old leading-coefficient functional $\ell$
by $w=\sqrt2$. Define
\[
 u=A^{-1}\ell,\quad h=(\ell^Tu)^{-1},\quad a=hu,\quad
 v=A^{-1}b,\quad \sigma=d-b^Tv,\quad \eta=\ell^Tv-w.
\]
Here $a$ is the old monic minimizer, and $h$ is its form norm; it is
not a ground eigenvalue.

\begin{proposition}[Joint monic mode update]
\label{prop:joint-mode-update}
Put $\mathcal D=\sigma+\eta^2h$, $q=\eta a-v$ and
$\tau_*=-\eta h/\mathcal D$. The new monic minimizer and norm are
\[
 a_{\rm new}=\binom a0+\tau_*\binom q1,
 \qquad h_{\rm new}=\frac{h\sigma}{\mathcal D},
 \qquad \log_{10}\frac h{h_{\rm new}}
       =\log_{10}\left(1+\frac{\eta^2h}{\sigma}\right).
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For a fixed last coefficient $\tau$, completing the square under
$\ell^Tx+w\tau=1$ gives $x=a(1+\eta\tau)-\tau v$ and the energy
$h(1+\eta\tau)^2+\sigma\tau^2$. Its minimum gives the formulas.
Also $\ell^Tq+w=0$, so the update has one lower polynomial degree.
\end{proof}

Set $s=(N+1)^2$ and $\kappa=\sqrt2\,sQ_N(s)$. In the new form let
$K_{\rm new}$ reproduce polynomials of degree at most $N$, and put
\[
 r(t)=\frac{K_{\rm new}(t,s)}{K_{\rm new}(s,s)},
 \qquad h_r=\frac1{K_{\rm new}(s,s)}.
\]
The vector $(q,1)/\kappa$ represents $r$: it has degree at most $N$,
value one at $s$, and is orthogonal to $(t-s)$ times every old
polynomial of degree less than $N$. Consequently
\[
 h_r=\frac{\mathcal D}{\kappa^2},\qquad
 P_{N+1}(t)=(t-s)P_N(t)-\xi_Nr(t),\qquad
 \xi_N=\frac{\eta h\kappa}{\mathcal D}.
\]
This identity holds at $t=s$ as well. If $K_{\rm old}$ reproduces
old degrees at most $N-1$, the orthogonal direct sum also gives
\[
 K_{\rm new}(t,u)=(t-s)(u-s)K_{\rm old}(t,u)
                         +\frac{r(t)r(u)}{h_r}.
\]
No global positive zeta measure is needed for these finite-form
identities. Under the earlier conditional measure interpretation,
$d\mu_{C,N}=(t-s)^2d\mu_{C,N+1}$ is consistent with the same
embedding; division alone could omit a mass at the removed carrier.

Write $g_N(t)=[\Gamma(N+1+\sqrt t)\Gamma(N+1-\sqrt t)]^{-1}$.
Since $g_N=(s-t)g_{N+1}$, the corresponding monic transforms satisfy
\[
 F_{N+1}(x)=F_N(x)+I_N(x),\qquad
 I_N(x)=(-1)^N\sqrt L\,\xi_Nr((x/\beta)^2)g_{N+1}((x/\beta)^2).
\]
The carrier change cancels the embedded part of the polynomial
exactly. The remaining update depends on $\eta$, $\sigma$ and $r$,
not merely on the Gamma factor. These identities pass 2275 exact
rational checks on 35 positive fixtures and 585 interval checks on
five existing $C=125$ prefixes, $N=10,20,40,80,120$. The latter use
2100 working digits and verified positive interval pivots. An initial
700-digit direct-inverse check failed arithmetic qualification at
$N=120$ and is retained separately.

At an old zero $x_N$, a constant-sign derivative bound
$|F_{N+1}'|\ge m_N>0$ on $[x_N-\rho,x_N+\rho]$, together with
$|I_N(x_N)|<m_N\rho$, gives a unique next zero and
$|x_{N+1}-x_N|\le |I_N(x_N)|/m_N$. A summable indexed majorant
would control the mode tail. Uniform parameter estimates, identification
of its limit, transfer to the changing ground shift, and the finite
precision remainder remain open. The exact monic norm formula must
not be substituted for the ground-eigenvalue convergence rate.
